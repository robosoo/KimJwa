## External dependencies to pin before any result upgrade

- Ramulator2 revision and configuration, DRAM standard/model and controller family.
- Native workload generator, full original request identity, address and R/W/arrival information.
- Issued-command stream and independent state/timing/bus legality checker.
- Waveform and PDN model family, SI/PI parameters, actuator charge/peak/RMS/slew budget and time horizon.
- Independent seeds and split definitions; unit of statistical independence.
- Exact original script versions, input hashes, outputs, figures and archive manifests.
- Legally redistributable licenses for any external trace and circuit data.

## Publication/privacy boundary

Public summaries can cite original artifact filenames and reported numbers while clearly flagging lack of independent rerun. Do not upload internal/company datasheets, hardware measurements, raw private archives, account information, third-party copyrighted PDFs, or manuscript text without rights review.
